Notes & Conditions

  1. Tuition for Year 2, 2023/2024 is set at KES 85,000 per year, payable in two equal semester instalments of KES 42,500. This rate applies to the 2022 intake only and is reviewed annually.
  2. Boarding is optional. Day scholars are not charged Room & Board or Meals — deduct KES 30,000 per semester from the gross total.
  3. HELB disbursement is subject to confirmation from the Higher Education Loans Board. If HELB funds are not received by the due date, the student is responsible for paying in full and claiming a refund once HELB remits.
  4. Fees paid are non-refundable except where a student withdraws before the second week of the semester, in which case the refund policy in the Student Handbook applies.
  5. Late payment beyond the due date attracts a 2% monthly surcharge on the outstanding balance, and may result in the student being barred from end-of-semester examinations.
  6. Amounts shown are in Kenya Shillings (KES). This structure covers Year 2, 2023/2024 only, and supersedes any previously issued fee schedule for this year of study.

This fee structure applies to Year 2, 2023/2024 only. Fees are reviewed annually and may change for subsequent years of study. For queries, contact the Finance Office at finance@rvit.ac.ke or +254 20 123 4567.

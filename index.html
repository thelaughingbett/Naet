The registry

Six modules, built around how a university actually operates — not a generic project-management shell with education vocabulary bolted on.

REG
Admissions & curriculum

Programmes, syllabus approval, and course scheduling — with per-programme approval so a course can be core for one degree and elective for another, without duplicating the catalogue.

  • Shared teaching slots across programmes, one lecturer assignment
  • Syllabus states from proposed through retired
EXM
Grading & examinations

Configurable weighting per course — 30/70 CAT-to-exam as the default, overridable per class — with automated timetabling, clash detection, and QR-verified admit cards.

  • Grades freeze at publish time, immune to later policy changes
  • Invigilator assignment with workload limits enforced
FIN
Fees & finance

Fee structures by class and session, statements, part-payments, and the registration hold that only lifts once an account clears — no separate finance office spreadsheet to reconcile.

  • Exam cards gate on account status automatically
RES
Residence & welfare

Hostel allocation by room capacity and gender, plus a grievance system that routes complaints — academic, harassment, facilities — to the right office and escalates automatically if it stalls.

  • Harassment cases route straight to the Dean of Students, skipping department level
SOC
Clubs & societies

Membership, leadership terms, and patron oversight for every registered society — recruitment drives, approvals, and events routed for Dean of Students sign-off.

LEC
Lecturer & staff console

Class lists, results entry, invigilation duty, and a live timetable — built for the person standing in front of a class, not repurposed admin tooling.

Built to be extended, not replaced.

Naet is a template, not a finished platform. The integration layer is generic and event-driven: every subsystem — payments, ERP sync, notifications, events, news — follows the same pattern. Core holds abstract contracts. Implementations live in contrib/. Register one class and it works.

ERP

Event-driven ERP integration

When something happens anywhere in the system, a dispatch fires on transaction commit. A Celery task resolves the instance, looks up the registered handler for that event, and runs it — with exponential backoff and per-handler retry up to five attempts before notifying admin.

# Event naming — model.action
payment.confirmed · enrollment.approved
payment.failed · reporting.submitted
deferment.created · result.published
student.graduated · student.registered
Sync to SAP, Oracle, a homegrown PHP system, or a Google Sheet — the layer doesn't care. Write one class extending AbstractERPTask, register it, done.
PLUGGABLE

Consistent contract across subsystems

Adding a new payment provider, notification channel, calendar source, or news feed follows the same three-step pattern everywhere. No provider-specific code ever lives in core.

  • payments
  • erp
  • notifications
  • events
  • news

Each subsystem exposes an abstract base class, a registry, and a reference implementation in contrib/. Local integrators write their backend alongside the base or in their own Django app — dynamic registration via apps.py ready() is supported so third-party packages stay isolated.

The rule that keeps it clean: core packages contain only abstract contracts and generic orchestration. Implementations go in contrib/ or the integrator's own app.
Open Source

Your university's record shouldn't be someone else's lock-in.

Naet is fully open source. Inspect every line that calculates a grade point average, audit how fee holds are applied, contribute features, or fork it for your own institution. No black boxes, no hidden fees.

  • § Self-hosted on your infrastructure Student records live on servers your institution controls — not in a vendor's multi-tenant cloud.
  • † No per-student licence fee No seat count, no renewal trap, no module gated behind a higher tier. Budget for hosting and support, not a headcount tax.
  • ‡ Fork it, extend it, own it Add the integrations your institution needs — an eLearning platform, a payment gateway, a national reporting feed — without waiting on a roadmap.
  • ◆ Auditable by design Registrars, ICT directors, and internal audit can inspect the exact logic behind versioning, freezing, and access control.

How a complaint actually moves

A grievance can't quietly sit on someone's desk.

Every complaint carries a category-specific SLA. If it isn't resolved before the clock runs out, it escalates automatically — priority bumped, next office notified — without anyone having to chase it.

  1. Department — Head of Department
    72h SLA
  2. School — Dean
    72h SLA
  3. Dean of Students
    48h SLA
  4. Registrar, Academic Affairs
    72h SLA
  5. University Senate
    final review

Deploying in Kenya means meeting Kenyan obligations.

Naet collects sensitive personal data — national IDs, financial records, family information, and special category data under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. Compliance isn't a checkbox; it's documented and reflected in the schema. Every regulator a deploying institution answers to is listed below, with the obligation it imposes.

ODPC Data protection registration Every deploying institution is a data controller under the DPA 2019 and must register with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner before going live.
CUE Academic records retention Universities Act, 2012 — admission and academic records retained permanently and exportable on audit request.
KRA 7-year financial retention Fee receipts, payment records, and ledgers retained per Kenya Revenue Authority requirements. Payment references preserved.
HELB Enrollment verification Semester reporting model supports HELB enrollment confirmation exports for loan beneficiaries.
KE-CIRT Cybercrime reporting Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018 — role-scoped admin, password hashing, and audit logging on the application side.
NCPWD Accessibility Persons with Disabilities Act — WCAG 2.1 AA as the target for the student-facing portal.
Full obligations, retention schedules, and breach response in DATA_PROTECTION.md and LEGAL.md.
